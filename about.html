About APU Tigers

Building champions and fostering excellence since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010, APU Tigers Basketball Club was born from a simple vision: to create a premier basketball community that develops not just exceptional athletes, but outstanding individuals.

What started with just 15 passionate students has grown into APU's most successful sports organization, boasting over 150 active members and 12 championship titles.

Today, we're proud to offer world-class facilities, professional coaching, and a supportive community that pushes every member to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To foster athletic excellence, teamwork, and personal growth through competitive basketball, while promoting health, wellness, and school spirit.

Our Vision

To be the leading university basketball organization in Southeast Asia.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do
